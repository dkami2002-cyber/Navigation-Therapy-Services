Our Philosophy

Every hero needs a guide

Long before maps, travelers found their way home by a fixed point of light, and a thread that led safely back out of the maze. That's the promise we carry into every session.

★
The guiding star

A fixed point to steer by

Sailors once crossed open water by keeping one star constant in the sky. We do the same with your child's goals — clear, individualized, and steady, session after session.

✺
Ariadne's thread

A way through, not just in

In the old story, a single thread led a hero safely out of a labyrinth. Our ABA, Speech, OT, and PT teams share one thread of notes and goals, so nothing gets lost between disciplines.

✦
The compass rose

Many directions, one true course

Four points, one instrument. Your family, your child, and our clinicians all read from the same compass — so everyone moves in the same direction, together.

What we offer

Four disciplines, one coordinated route

Each service area is a bearing on the same compass — our teams share notes and goals so your child's plan stays connected, whatever direction their care takes.

Two ways to meet your team

Care that meets you where you are

Some sessions are best face to face; others fit better into a busy week from home. Both are full, supported visits with the same care team.

⌂

In-person visits

Hands-on sessions at one of our clinic locations, with the full range of therapy tools and equipment on hand.

  • Best for ABA, feeding therapy, and PT/OT skill-building
  • Two clinic locations, both wheelchair accessible
  • Waiting area support for siblings and family
◐

Virtual visits

Live video sessions from any device — no downloads required. Ideal for consults, parent coaching, and select therapy sessions.

  • Best for parent coaching & ABA supervision
  • Speech, OT and PT consults and check-ins
  • Notes posted to your portal right after
